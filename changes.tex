Action frequency: Change action_freq from 20 to 60, making each RL step cover 30 simulated seconds instead of 10. This ensures that by the time the reward is computed, a commanded turn has physically completed in BlueSky. The policy can then properly observe the full geometric consequence of its action — a real CPA improvement — rather than a half-finished turn that looks nearly identical to doing nothing.
Reward shaping: Replace the current threshold-based safety penalty with a continuous exponential decay over CPA distance. The current formula produces zero gradient whenever the predicted CPA exceeds 10 NM, which is most of the time given typical aircraft spacing. An exponential gives a small but non-zero penalty even when aircraft are far apart and converging, meaning the policy receives a learning signal throughout the entire approach of a conflict — not just in the final dangerous window.
