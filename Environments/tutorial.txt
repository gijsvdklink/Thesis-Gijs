EXPERIMENT 1 -- action-response delays in RL-based conflict resolution

Three models, one variable: how long an instruction takes to reach the aircraft.
All three run the SAME environment (Environments/v4_experiments) so any difference is
attributable to the delay alone. Keep --seed identical across the three arms.

    arm             first advisory        subsequent advisory
    none            0 s                   0 s
    deterministic   25 s                  12.5 s
    probabilistic   log-normal, mean 25 s log-normal, mean 12.5 s

"Subsequent" means the pilot is already engaged, so it applies only AFTER an advisory has
actually EXECUTED in the current urgency cycle. Re-issuing while an instruction is still
outstanding is an amendment: it replaces the action but inherits the original deadline.

Environments/v4 is NOT part of this experiment -- it is the archived normalised env.

WHAT TO WATCH IN TENSORBOARD

  safety/los_per_fh        losses of separation per flight-hour. Reference point: 0.691
                           under a do-nothing policy at the current traffic density.
  safety/los_events        LoS entries (one long intrusion = one event), not steps in LoS.

  arrival/on_route         lenient: heading error to route <= 5 deg at exit, i.e. the
                           aircraft is flying straight at its destination again.
  arrival/xtrack           middle: lateral offset from the intended route line <= 15 NM.
  arrival/ref              strict: within 5 NM of the reference point.
                           All three exclude aircraft that exited before really flying.

  strategy/tlos_at_issue   THE anticipation metric. Mean time-to-LoS when an instruction is
                           issued. If delayed agents learn to act earlier, this rises.
  strategy/turn_magnitude  mean |heading change| per instruction: do they also turn harder?
  delay/amend_lead_s       how long before execution amendments arrive. An agent that learns
                           to issue early and amend for free will show a large value here.
  delay/pending_frac       fraction of steps with an instruction outstanding.
  delay/mean_delay_s       realised mean delay; sanity-check (near 25 s early on, falling
                           toward 12.5 s as more cycles reach a second advisory).
