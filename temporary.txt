\noindent{\large\bfseries Edge definitions}\\[3pt]
{\footnotesize Solid edges are transitions internal to a single agent; dashed
edges carry data between agents.}\\[10pt]
{\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{@{}c l l l@{}}
\toprule
& \textbf{From $\rightarrow$ to} & \textbf{Trigger} & \textbf{Effect} \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{BlueSky $\rightarrow$ CR tool (data)}}\\
A & Airspace $\rightarrow$ Construct $U$ & CR-tool cycle begins & States of all live aircraft are read \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Conflict-resolution tool (internal)}}\\
B & Construct $U$ $\rightarrow$ Determine focus ship & $U$ assembled & Most urgent conflict pair chosen, and held \\
C & Determine focus ship $\rightarrow$ Build observation & Focus ship $i$ fixed & Observation vector $o$ assembled \\
D & Delay $\rightarrow$ Build observation (data) & Focus ship selected & Whether advice is outstanding, and for how long \\
E & Build observation $\rightarrow$ Trained model & $o$ complete & $o$ passed to the trained model \\
F & Trained model $\rightarrow$ no action taken & $a=\varnothing$, or $a$ already flown & Cycle ends without advice \\
G & Trained model $\rightarrow$ New advice (data) & $a\neq\varnothing$ & Advisory handed to the ATCO \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{ATCO (internal)}}\\
H & No advice $\rightarrow$ New advice & Advisory received & Aircraft taken up \\
I & New advice $\rightarrow$ Begin response & Aircraft taken up & $\tau\sim D(\mu)$ drawn, or $p=1/\mu$ begun \\
J & Begin response $\rightarrow$ Delay & Response begun & Clock running \\
K & Delay $\rightarrow$ Delay & Advisory arrives while waiting & Instruction replaced; the clock is \emph{not} restarted \\
L & Delay $\rightarrow$ No advice & Instruction passed on & ATCO free again \\
R & Delay $\rightarrow$ New advice & Advisory for a \emph{different} aircraft & Instruction in hand dropped unflown; new $\tau$ \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{ATCO $\rightarrow$ BlueSky}}\\
M & Delay $\rightarrow$ BS executes instruction (data) & $\tau$ elapsed, or $p$ fires & Heading or speed command handed to BlueSky \\
N & BS executes instruction $\rightarrow$ Airspace & Every simulation step & Heading and position integrated forward \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textit{Traffic generation}}\\
O & Spawn $\rightarrow$ Airspace & Arrival process fires & Aircraft enters the sector \\
P & Airspace $\rightarrow$ Delete & Sector boundary crossed & Aircraft leaves and is removed \\
Q & Delete $\rightarrow$ Spawn & Removal complete & Traffic density held constant \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}}
